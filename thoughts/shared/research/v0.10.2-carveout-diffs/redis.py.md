## Risks / follow-ups

1. **Dead alias**: `parse_redis_service_url = parse_redis_url` (row 4) has no remaining callers in the codebase per grep — harmless, but could be removed in a future cleanup pass if desired. Not a correctness issue.
2. **Two lookalike aliases with different provenance**: `parse_redis_service_url` (fork-added, unused) vs `get_sentinel_url_from_env` (fork-added, **actively used** by `socket/main.py`). Worth remembering these are asymmetric in importance if either is ever "cleaned up" — deleting `get_sentinel_url_from_env` would be a real crash risk for HA/Sentinel deployments; deleting `parse_redis_service_url` would not.
3. **`_CONNECTION_CACHE`/`_LIFESPAN_FLUSHED` mechanism is untouched by upstream** and remains the single most important piece of fork-specific logic in this file — any future upstream merge touching `get_redis_connection()` should re-verify this warning/flush logic survives intact, since upstream has no awareness of it and could plausibly clobber it in a future full-file rewrite the way this one nearly did.
4. No action items block this merge from the `redis.py` side — nearly all upstream refactor/bugfix work was successfully ported, and the two intentional divergences (`_CONNECTION_CACHE` naming, HA loop-safety warning) are both correctly preserved and load-bearing for the fork's HA story.
